Maximize Your Restaurant's Potential

Discover how our intelligent digital menus increase your average order value and eliminate commissions.

Can my customers download this like a real mobile app?
Yes, absolutely! With our built-in App Installation technology, customers visiting your digital menu can install it directly to their smartphone's home screen with a single tap. It appears exactly like a native app, complete with your restaurant's official brand logo, sitting right next to their other daily apps. This keeps your restaurant top-of-mind for their next meal!
How does "Direct WhatsApp Ordering" actually work?
It is a complete game-changer that saves you from 30% delivery app commissions. When a customer finishes building their cart, the system doesn't just send a basic text. It instantly generates a professional, fully-formatted digital receipt sent straight to your restaurant's WhatsApp. It includes complete item details, quantities, precise date & time, Dine-in or Takeaway preferences, table numbers, and a complete price breakdown including VAT charges.
Which plan is right for my menu size?
Whether you run a cozy cafe or a massive multi-cuisine restaurant, we have advanced lightning-fast catalog for you. The Starter Plan is perfect for highly focused menus up to 50 items. The Professional Plan expands your capacity to 150 items, while our Premium Plan easily manages massive catalogs of up to 300 items without ever slowing down.
How do Banners, Flash Cards, and "Today's Special" boost sales?
Visuals sell! The Today's Special section allows you to rotate and highlight your everyday signature dishes right at the top of the menu so they cannot be missed. Furthermore, our eye-catching Category Banners and Flash Cards are awesome tools to display irresistible promotional offers, seasonal menus, or limited-time discounts, driving massive customer engagement.
What is the "Frequently Bought Together" feature?
Think of it as your automated, silent up-seller. This smart feature tags related items together—like seamlessly suggesting your famous garlic bread and a cold drink the moment a customer adds a pizza to their cart. It effortlessly tempts customers to add "just one more thing," safely increasing your average check size on every order!
