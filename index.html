The real story behind every missed order

Answering the same question, twenty times a day

Most small businesses aren't losing sales to bad food or bad service — they're losing time and patience to the gap between a customer's question and a clear answer.

Every call, twenty questions

"What's the price?" "How much does it weigh?" "Is it in stock?" Staff spend the day repeating answers instead of taking the order.

Explained twice, still unclear

A server walks the table through the whole menu, and the guest still asks to see it again. Words alone rarely sell a dish.

Printed menus go stale fast

Whether it's a simple or a luxury printed menu, a price rises or an item sells out — and the card in front of the guest still tells yesterday's story.

Rush hour turns into chaos

Phones ring nonstop right when tables need attention most, orders get scribbled down, and mistakes multiply exactly when you can least afford them.

Solve these four moments and everything else gets easier. So that's exactly where Menuly starts — before we ever talk about the advanced features.

Questions

Everything you're probably wondering

Click a question to expand it — the rest will tuck themselves away.

Do my customers need to download an app?
No. Menuly opens instantly in any phone browser from a QR code or link — no app store, no download. Guests can also add it to their home screen with one tap if they want quick access next time.
How does WhatsApp ordering actually work?
When a guest finishes their order, Menuly generates a clean, fully-formatted message — items, quantities, price breakdown, table number or delivery details — and sends it straight to your restaurant or shop's WhatsApp. No missed calls, no handwriting to decode.
Which plan fits my business size?
The Starter plan suits a focused menu up to 50 items. Professional covers up to 150. Premium handles catalogs up to 300 items with the full feature set — specials, reviews, and the game arcade included.
Can I collect Google and social media reviews through the app?
Yes. Right after checkout, guests see a friendly one-tap prompt linking straight to your Google Business profile or social page, while the experience is still fresh in their mind.
What happens while customers wait for their order?
Instead of staring at the door, guests can browse specials, revisit favorites, or open the built-in mini-game arcade — keeping the wait pleasant and your brand front of mind.
